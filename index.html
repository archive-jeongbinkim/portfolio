Jeongbin Kim is a designer working across editorial, visual identity, creative direction, painting, photography, graphic design, and typography. Her projects are developed both independently and through collaborations with strangers. Her practice is grounded in an approach centered on visual and conceptual thinking, aiming for project-specific aesthetics and tailored solutions. With a double major in fine art and design, she navigates the boundary between the two disciplines, bringing a distinct sensibility to her work.
